An optional bridge between ``content_center_base`` and ``contact_center_ui``.
Agents consult company information, product documents, media, links and FAQs from
the conversation composer and add shareable content to their current draft.

Searches and downloads require catalog read access and access to the selected
conversation. Content is restricted to the conversation company, even when the
agent has several active companies. Sharing rechecks availability and the editor's
shareable flag. Original binary attachments stay owned by their catalog items;
the existing authenticated download/upload flow creates conversation copies.

No public catalog URLs or editorial approval workflow are introduced. This bridge
does not install sales, attribution, advertising providers or AI modules. It is
installed explicitly when content sharing in Contact Center is needed.

Installation
============

Direct Odoo dependencies: ``content_center_base``, ``contact_center_ui``.

Configuration
=============

Both repositories must be on the Odoo addons path: ``content-center`` for the
catalog and ``contact-center`` for ``contact_center_ui``. Install this optional
bridge and add the **Content Center / Reader** group to agents who need catalog access.
They also need access to their Contact Center accounts and conversations.

Editors maintain company-specific subjects and content in the catalog app. Mark
items **Can Share with Customer** to make them selectable in the conversation
popup. Internal items remain available for consultation only.

Usage
=====

Open the book button beside the message field. The popup first shows subject
cards for company information and solutions. Search by name, content or product
reference, or filter by a specific product/variant. Open a subject to browse
**Information**, **Materials** and **FAQ**. Image cards, inline PDF previews and
supported video previews let agents inspect a file before adding it.

Select shareable items across subjects and tabs. The selection remains available
while navigating the catalog. **Add to message** appends text and prepares
independent file copies in the current draft. Send through the regular Contact
Center composer when ready. Existing draft text is preserved. Internal content
can be consulted and downloaded, but cannot be selected for sharing.

A failed file upload uses the Contact Center's regular retry/error handling. Items
that reach the draft, including failed upload cards, are removed from the catalog
selection. Retry failed uploads in the composer; it reuses the existing card.
Editing or archiving a library entry does not synchronize an already prepared
message. Account/provider limits and the regular composer attachment limit apply.

Diagnosis
~~~~~~~~~

If the catalog control is unavailable, check that this bridge is installed,
the agent has Catalog Reader access, and the selected account/conversation is
accessible. Missing items can result from company, product, archive or sharing
filters. A prepared file is a conversation attachment copy: retry its upload in
the composer rather than adding another copy from the catalog. No message is
sent by **Add to message**; sending remains the agent's normal composer action.
This bridge does not create acquisition touchpoints, leads or UTM mappings.
