Content Center

A shared, editable library of company and product information for Odoo 16. Marketing and engineering maintain subjects containing formatted text, FAQs, files and external links. Sales and support can consult the same current source.

This standalone application does not require the Marketing Center analytics suite. Optional addons connect the library to Sales and Contact Center.

  • Save content directly, edit it in place and archive it when no longer useful.
  • Keep structured information and frequently asked questions together in each subject.
  • Link subjects to product templates, individual variants or both.
  • Restrict individual items to specific products when necessary.
  • Distinguish internal information from content that can be shared with customers.
  • Keep files private and isolate each company’s library.

There is no editorial approval, publishing workflow, immutable revision archive, public portal or AI service in this initial version.

Installation

Direct Odoo dependencies: product, mail, web, web_editor.

This standalone content library does not depend on marketing_center_base. It is distinct from the external advertising-entity catalog and native UTM records.

Configuration

In Settings / Users, grant Content Center / Editor to the people maintaining the library and Content Center / Reader to the people consulting it. Administrators inherit editor access. Catalog access is separate from sales, support and advertising analytics roles.

Each subject belongs to one company. Linked products must either belong to that company or be shared products. Company record rules apply to both subjects and content, including downloads through Odoo’s authenticated binary route.

Usage

Open Content Center / Subjects and create a company or solution subject. Use the Information tab for Structured Information and, directly below it, Frequently Asked Questions. Use Materials for one file per item or external HTTP(S) links. Saving makes content available immediately. Mark items as shareable only when they may be sent to customers.

Structured Information, Frequently Asked Questions and Materials each offer independent List and Kanban views. Information and questions start in List; Materials starts in Kanban. Use the view buttons to change the layout while working on the same subject.

The Frequently Asked Questions list shows only Question and Answer. Add or edit them directly in the list; answers support formatted text. New FAQs use the question as their title when no title was supplied. Existing custom titles are preserved. Switch to Kanban and open a card to edit additional settings, such as sharing permissions.

Link a product template to include all its variants. Linking one variant only includes that variant. Leave subject product links empty for information that applies to all products, such as company information. Item product restrictions intersect with the subject links; they never broaden the subject’s scope. When no product is selected, all items remain discoverable and show applicability.

The Content button on templates and variants opens a company-scoped library with removable product filters. Start with the subject cards, then browse Information, Materials and FAQ. Readers can open previews, download files and copy text. Archive an item or subject to remove it from library searches. Archived records remain accessible to authorized users through the regular management screens.

Optional integrations:

  • content_center_sale adds the library to quotations and orders.
  • content_center_contact_center adds content to a conversation draft, using independent attachment copies and the normal message composer.

Editing or archiving the library does not rewrite previously prepared drafts or sent messages. Native record identifiers and modification dates support later integrations, without promising immutable historical sources.

Subjects and the content library open as native kanban cards. Uploaded photos render directly; PDF cards show the first page when visible, and opening a PDF shows the document viewer. Supported uploaded videos play on demand. Other files remain downloadable. External links open in another tab.

Photos, PDF documents and videos remain ordinary File items: their preview format is detected automatically. The optional material category describes the purpose (photo, catalog, datasheet, manual and so on). Materials only offers File or Link; Structured Information creates text and Frequently Asked Questions creates questions and answers, both within the Information tab.

Diagnosis

If content is missing, check the selected company, Catalog Reader/Editor access, archive state and current product filters. Review both subject-level product links and item restrictions; clear the product filter to distinguish missing content from an applicability restriction. If a file cannot be shared, check its Can Share with Customer visibility and the optional integration’s own access rules. The library does not publish public download links or preserve immutable editorial revisions.

Bug Tracker

Bugs are tracked on GitHub Issues. In case of trouble, please check there if your issue has already been reported. If you spotted it first, help us to smash it by providing a detailed and welcomed feedback.

Do not contact contributors directly about support or help with technical issues.
